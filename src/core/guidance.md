# MzansiRide SafeAI — approved safety guidance

This is the ONLY information the assistant may use for facts about the app, procedures and phone numbers.
Last reviewed: 2026-10-24 by the MzansiRide team. Numbers sourced from SA government (sanews.gov.za,
justice.gov.za, vukuzenzele.gov.za) and the GBV Command Centre (gbv.org.za).

## Emergency contacts in South Africa
- Immediate danger: call SAPS on **10111**, or **112** from any mobile phone (works without airtime).
- Ambulance / medical emergency: **10177**.
- GBV Command Centre (24 hours, free): **0800 428 428**. "Please call me": dial **\*120\*7867#**.
  People with hearing or speech disabilities can SMS **"help" to 31531**.
- The assistant and the app do NOT contact emergency services. The passenger must call them.

## What the app's safety alerts mean
- **Prolonged stop**: the vehicle has not moved for a few minutes away from the pickup or destination.
  Common harmless reasons: traffic, a red light, roadworks, a load-shedding outage at traffic lights, a
  passenger drop-off on a shared ride, fuel, or the driver taking a call safely parked.
- **Route deviation**: the vehicle is noticeably away from the planned route. Common harmless reasons:
  a road closure, an accident ahead, traffic avoidance, or a navigation app choosing another road.
- An alert is a **check-in prompt**, not proof that anything is wrong. The app never assumes the
  driver is acting maliciously and does not predict crime.

## Check-in options in the app
1. **"I'm OK"**: the alert is closed and recorded. Nobody is contacted.
2. **"I need help"**: the alert is escalated and the passenger's trusted contact is sent the trip
   details, including the last known location, driver name and vehicle registration.
3. **No answer within about 2 minutes** (trip time): the alert is escalated automatically and the trusted
   contact is notified.
- The passenger can also use **Share trip** at any time, and **SOS** on WhatsApp.

## Practical safety tips (general guidance)
- Before getting in: check that the number plate, car model and colour, and driver name match the app.
  On WhatsApp, send `CHECK <plate>` to look the vehicle up in the MzansiRide registry.
- Sit in the back seat, keep the phone charged, and keep the trip shared with a trusted contact.
- If the route changes, it is reasonable to ask the driver politely why, for example "Are we avoiding
  traffic?".
- If you feel unsafe: ask to be dropped at a busy, well-lit public place such as a petrol station or mall,
  contact your trusted contact, and call 10111 or 112 if you are in danger.
- Trust your instincts. You may end a trip early when it is safe to do so.

## WhatsApp commands
- `HELP`: shows the menu. `CHECK <plate or driver name>`: driver/vehicle registry lookup.
- `TRIP <code>`: link this chat to a trip to receive check-in prompts. `1` = I'm OK, `2` = I need help.
- `SOS`: notifies the trusted contact on the linked trip. Any other message is answered by the AI assistant.

## Limits (always be honest about these)
- This is a hackathon prototype. Trips, drivers, vehicles and alerts are SIMULATED demo data.
- Driver records come from a demo registry. They are not an official police, licensing or criminal
  record check.
- The assistant can make mistakes and does not know whether a passenger is safe.
